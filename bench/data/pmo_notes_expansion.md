**Supervisor:** Alex Rivera, PMO Director
**Team:** Maria Chen (Senior PM), Devon Walker (PM), Priya Nair (Business Analyst), Jordan Lee (Project Coordinator), Sam Okafor (Scheduler / Resource Analyst)
**Entry count:** 50 (Part A: 28, Part B: 22)

**How this fits with the first set:** The first file covers Jul 2 – Sep 25, 2026. Part A below is the prequel (Jan 6 – Jun 30) and Part B is the sequel (Sep 28 – Oct 20). Pasted in date order, the three pieces form one continuous log.

**New names used:** Dana Whitfield (VP of Operations, Alex's boss), Kestrel Systems (Meridian's vendor), Halcyon Health (Devon's client), PortfolioView (new portfolio tool), Tomás (contractor, mentioned in text only), Northwind Logistics (the firm that makes Sam an offer).

**Intentional test features:**
- Typos and sloppy grammar in many entries, worst in the quick "random thought" ones.
- Entries with no single employee (Team-wide / Self-note).
- Entries marked `Visibility: Private`, to test privacy handling.
- Longer entries, with some same-day and multi-person events.
- Alex's own notes drift in tone (more words about people he's worried about), which is a pattern a good tool should surface.

**Loose plot:**
1. Sam's resourcing workbook is a single point of failure, and Sam is quietly burning out. It ends on a cliffhanger over whether he stays.
2. Maria and Priya are both ambitious. A PMO Lead role is quietly being considered, and a leak puts them in light competition.
3. Devon's charm is his strength and his blind spot (verbal commitments, thin documentation). He improves, then overextends.
4. Jordan is hired, grows steadily, and takes on a solo project.
5. Alex has to be honest with people while holding a confidence from Dana. He also realizes his own notes are biased toward whoever worries him.
