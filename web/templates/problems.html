Why so little detail

What actually failed is not shown, here or on the error page itself. The message can carry a file path or a piece of a note, and note text does not belong anywhere the encryption does not reach — a log file most of all. What is kept is when it happened, which page you were on, and the kind of fault. The rest lives in the service log, under the same reference.
